# Wyld Wandering
## Identity study 01 — October 22, 2026

Initial creative proposal. Generated raster concepts for review; final vector masters and print-ready templates are not included.

### Direction
A warm editorial identity for personal travel planning, drawing on Zak's luxury hospitality experience. The intertwined WW monogram suggests a winding journey; the serif wordmark gives it the character of a distinctive independent hotel. The visual language should feel personal, assured and curious.

### Logo system
- Primary: the WW monogram centered above the title-case name, Wyld Wandering.
- Secondary: standalone WW for social profiles and compact applications.
- Reverse treatment: warm ivory on forest.
- Keep the symbol and wordmark proportions intact. Do not stretch, outline, add effects or set over busy photography.
- Proposed clear space: at least half the monogram height around a complete lockup, and one quarter of its width around a standalone mark.
- Small-size guidance requires testing after vector refinement; use the full name where the audience may not recognize WW.

### Color specifications
These hex values are the intended production targets. Generated artwork and mockups may vary slightly; do not sample the pictures to establish the palette.

| Color | Hex | Role |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Forest | #0F1A14 | Main logo, dark surfaces, body text |
| Ivory | #FFFBF5 | Main background and reverse logo |
| Terracotta | #C45D3A | Occasional accent, cards and highlights |
| Amber | #D4A857 | Fine rules and small decorative details |

Lead with forest and ivory. Use terracotta selectively and amber sparingly. Keep small text forest on ivory or ivory on forest.

### Typography
The generated wordmark is a visual lettering concept, not an identified or packaged typeface. Refine it as custom vector artwork before final production.

Continue the existing site's Cormorant display and Outfit body pairing for supporting communications. Cormorant Medium/Semibold suits editorial headings; Outfit Regular/Medium suits itinerary details, navigation and captions. Keep paragraphs comfortably readable and reserve letterspacing for short labels. Font files are not bundled.

### Voice
Direct, personal and knowledgeable. Emphasize the right hotel, thoughtful details and advice grounded in hospitality experience. Avoid generic luxury superlatives or guarantees about upgrades and access.

Existing headline retained: “Your trip, handled.”
Proposed supporting phrase: “Places worth finding.”
Descriptor: “Personal travel planning.”

### Applications
The board shows an itinerary cover, business-card concept and circular social-avatar treatment. These are visual mockups, not editable or print-ready templates. For photography, favor distinctive places, architectural details and a sense of being there. Give images room to breathe.

### Production handoff
The next design stage is to settle the direction, refine the WW curves and lettering into a single vector master, and derive every variation from that master. Generated variants have minor shape and color differences. Export SVG/PDF/PNG lockups and an optically adjusted small icon, then verify legibility at actual use sizes and prepare editable stationery/social templates with final content.

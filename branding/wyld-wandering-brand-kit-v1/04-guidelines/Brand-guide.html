WYLD WANDERING   /   PLACEMENT AND SIZE

Room to breathe.

Keep at least one quarter of the monogram’s height clear around the visible artwork. For the wordmark alone, use one quarter of the lettering height. Measure from the artwork, not the file edge.

Place the mark on a quiet, high-contrast surface. Keep the original proportions and approved color.

Recommended minimum widths

AssetScreenPrint
Stacked220 px45 mm
Horizontal280 px60 mm
Wordmark200 px45 mm
Monogram48 px12 mm

Use the heavier small-symbol artwork for 16–47 px web icons. Always review at actual size; fine strokes need more space on absorbent paper or textured materials.

Keep the artwork intact

No stretching, rotation, shadows, gradients, outlines or rearranged lettering. Do not recreate the wordmark by typing the brand name.

Lead with forest and ivory

Use forest text on ivory, or ivory text on forest. Reserve terracotta for emphasis and amber for fine details. Avoid amber text on ivory.

Use the supplied values

All master artwork uses exact sRGB colors. The package includes CSS, JSON and a GIMP palette. For print, have the printer convert from the vector PDF using the profile for the selected paper and process.

WYLD WANDERING   /   VOICE AND IMAGERY

Speak like someone who knows.

The voice

Personal, direct and informed. Explain why a place is worth a client’s time and what makes it right for that particular trip.

Use concrete details: the room, the neighborhood, the morning walk, the reservation. Keep confidence grounded in what is known or confirmed.

Core language

Your trip, handled.

Primary headline

Places worth finding.

Supporting editorial phrase

Descriptor: Personal travel planning.

Photography

Choose distinctive places, human-scale architecture, natural light and considered details. Favor a sense of being there over a catalog of attractions.

Allow one strong image to lead. Keep horizons straight, color believable and space around typography. Use only imagery you own or have permission to use.

Writing example

A quiet courtyard, a walkable neighborhood, and a room worth lingering in.

This is illustrative style copy. Use property-specific details only after confirming them.

Avoid generic superlatives and promises of upgrades, access or availability unless confirmed for the booking.

WYLD WANDERING   /   HANDOFF

Use the right file.

01 Logos

SVG: scalable artwork for design and web.
PDF: vector artwork for placement and print.
PNG: transparent images for everyday use.

02 Social

Finished brand cards, avatars, banners and web icons. SVG source and PNG exports are paired by filename.

03 Templates

Five PowerPoint files covering social, itineraries and client presentations. Individual SVG layouts are also editable. Print layouts include PDF proofs.

04 Guidelines and 05 Source

This guide, fonts and licenses, palette files, master monogram and wordmark artwork, and provenance notes.

Business cards

Trim: 3.5 × 2 inches. Bleed: 0.125 inch on every edge. Artwork size: 3.75 × 2.25 inches. Keep live content at least 0.125 inch inside the trim. PDF TrimBox and BleedBox are supplied.

Print workflow

The PDFs are RGB vector artwork. Confirm the printer’s preferred color conversion and stock, then review a proof. The itinerary and letterhead use US Letter dimensions.

Editing workflow

Open PPTX in PowerPoint or import it into your slide editor. Open SVG in a vector editor; text remains editable. Logos are fixed artwork in the slide templates; the SVG masters allow path editing.
